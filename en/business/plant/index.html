Power Plant & Facility Construction

From solar plant construction to safety and maintenance after completion

One company stays with you from feasibility review and permitting through construction, grid connection and operation after completion.

Starting in Gwangyang, we now carry out sites nationwide with partners in Daegu and Hwaseong. Both self-consumption and power-sales types are possible, and for land-based solar we design configurations linked with ESS that go beyond transmission-line constraints.

Completion is only the beginning. That is why 「plant safety management and maintenance」 is listed among our registered business items.

Who this suits

· Business owners who want to build a plant on idle land, factory rooftops or parking lots

· Factories that need self-consumption systems to cut electricity bills

· Solar for public institutions, schools and public parking lots

Key Features

01

End-to-end delivery

Feasibility review → permitting → design → construction → grid connection → operation, all through a single point of contact.

02

ESS-linked design

In areas where KEPCO transmission lines are short of capacity, we design ESS together to store and transmit the generated power.

03

Maintenance after completion

We support generation monitoring, periodic safety inspections and fault response within the scope of the contract.
